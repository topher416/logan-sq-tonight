# Make the Site Actually Useful for Us

## What We're Building

Logan Square Tonight currently surfaces events but most are DJ sets that Topher and Cassie will never attend. The site needs to become a personal discovery tool that highlights the stuff they'd actually do, expands venue coverage, and surfaces recurring events they can build habits around.

## The Problem

Discovery is broken. Do312 is 95% noise. Venue websites require remembering to check. No Instagram means missing pop-ups, tastings, and community events entirely. The result: bored evenings in one of Chicago's best neighborhoods.

## What They Actually Go To

- Comedy shows (Lincoln Lodge, etc.)
- Trivia / pub quiz nights
- Live music (bands, jazz, singer-songwriter -- NOT DJs)
- Theater / improv
- Outdoor stuff (markets, park movie nights)
- Food/drink events (pop-ups, tastings, supper clubs) -- want to find these but currently not looped in

## Key Decisions

1. **Don't hide DJs, deprioritize them**: Visual hierarchy that makes "your kind of thing" pop while DJs fade into the background. Knowing there's a DJ at Whistler is fine -- it just shouldn't compete for attention with trivia night.

2. **Expand the neighborhood**: Logan Square is the core, but Bucktown, Wicker Park, maybe Humboldt Park are in range -- especially for theater/comedy.

3. **Highlight recurring events**: "Every Tuesday trivia at X" is the most valuable kind of event. Weekly/recurring events help build routines and meet neighbors.

4. **More venues**: Middle Brow is a must-add. Need to research what else is missing in Logan Square + adjacent neighborhoods for comedy, theater, trivia, live music.

## Feature Ideas (Unranked)

- **"For You" visual treatment**: Boost event types they care about (comedy, trivia, live-music, theater, outdoor, food-event) with stronger visual presence. DJ/generic bar events get muted styling.
- **Recurring event badges**: Tag events that happen weekly/monthly so they can spot habits to build.
- **Venue expansion**: Add venues in Bucktown/Wicker Park -- comedy clubs, theaters, bars with trivia nights, Middle Brow, etc.
- **Event type icons or tags**: Make it scannable at a glance -- comedy mask, music note, question mark for trivia, etc.
- **Weekend view**: Collapse Fri/Sat/Sun into one view since weekend planning is different from "tonight."

## Out of Scope

- Not a city-wide aggregator
- Not trying to be Do312
- No corny "things to do in Chicago" energy
- No social features or accounts

## Open Questions

- What specific venues to add in Bucktown/Wicker Park? (Research needed)
- How to discover recurring events programmatically vs. manually tagging?
- Should food/drink pop-ups come from Instagram scraping, or is there a better source?
- Does Middle Brow have a scrapable events page?
